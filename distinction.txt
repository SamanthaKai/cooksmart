INDIVIDUAL ACCOUNT

Who is this? 
Someone cooking at home — a student, a parent, someone managing a health condition, someone who just loves Ugandan food.

What they care about:
- What can I cook with what I have?
- Is this food good for my health condition?
- What should I eat this week?

Their dashboard focuses on:
- Personal meal planning (breakfast, lunch, dinner for the week)
- Health-centered AI features — dietary customization for diabetes, hypertension, allergies
- Saved and liked recipes
- AI recipe generation from available ingredients
- Shopping list for personal grocery runs

How they pay:
- Free tier: browse recipes, basic search
- Premium (UGX 15,000/month): unlimited AI generation, health insights, meal planning, dietary customization

---

ESTABLISHMENT ACCOUNT

Who is this?
A restaurant, hotel, catering company, school canteen, or food vendor.

What they care about:
- Are all my cooks making the same dish the same way?
- Can I train new staff quickly?
- Can I manage my own recipes alongside the standard ones?

Their dashboard focuses on:
- Staff accounts — one admin account, multiple staff logins under one establishment
- Recipe library management — add, edit, and standardize their own house recipes alongside the CookSmart database
- Portion scaling for bulk — scale recipes for 50, 100, 200 servings
- Weekly menu planning — plan the full restaurant menu by day
- Bulk shopping list — aggregated ingredient order list for the week
- Consistency tracking — which recipes are being used most, which staff accessed what
- Customer health feature — when a customer mentions a dietary need, staff can pull up an adapted recipe instantly

How they pay:
- Starter (UGX 50,000/month): up to 3 staff accounts, full AI features, recipe library
- Business (UGX 150,000/month): unlimited staff, bulk scaling, analytics, priority support
- Enterprise: custom pricing for hotel chains and institutions
